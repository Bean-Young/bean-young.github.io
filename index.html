Yuezhe Yang

I am currently a PhD student at the School of Computer Science, The University of Sydney. I am also a research intern at the Institute of Translational Medicine, SJTU. I received my B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from Anhui University. I was previously an intern at the Vision and Learning Lab at the University of Alberta and at the Anhui Provincial International Joint Research Center for Advanced Technology in Medical Imaging. My research is dedicated to the application of artificial intelligence in medicine.

I am actively seeking internship and collaboration opportunities in medical AI. We collaborate with hospitals, companies, and universities to advance translational medical research. I am open to all ideas, so please feel free to contact me if you are interested in my projects.
